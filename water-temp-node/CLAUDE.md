# water-temp-node/

- `water-temp-node/` — **PlatformIO / Arduino (STM32duino)** firmware for a **NUCLEO-WL55JC1** (STM32WL55JC, `board = nucleo_wl55jc`, `framework = arduino`, flash via onboard/external **ST-Link**). A **battery-powered LoRa** multi-probe **water-temperature** (up to 6) + **air/CO2** sensor node — the repo's **first non-ESP, first LoRa, first sub-GHz** device. **Why `arduino` not `stm32cube`:** PlatformIO's ststm32 platform has **no** stm32cube framework for the STM32WL family (no `framework-stm32cubewl` package exists in the official *or* maxgerhardt platforms; the WL board advertises only `arduino`/`zephyr`). But **STM32duino *is* the STM32Cube HAL underneath**, so the intended HAL SubGHz driver compiles unchanged — we just enable `HAL_SUBGHZ` via `include/hal_conf_extra.h` (the STM32duino override hook). Every `WAKE_INTERVAL_S` (default 15 min) it wakes from **Stop2** (raw-HAL `HAL_PWREx_EnterSTOP2Mode` + RTC/LSE wake on the CM4 `RTC_WKUP_IRQn` — **not** the STM32LowPower lib, whose 2.0.0 release `#error`s against the core PlatformIO bundles), powers **6× DS18B20** (one per GPIO, SKIP-ROM, so they convert in PARALLEL — one 750 ms wait, not six; P0=PA5 P1=PA4 P2=PA9 P3=PC2 P4=**PB8** P5=PB10 — **P4 moved off PC1 in 2026-08**, which is now LPUART1_TX for the S88) through an **AO3401A P-MOSFET** high-side gate (PA8, active-low, whole rail incl. pull-ups; 100k gate pull-up = OFF while Hi-Z), reads both temps (12-bit, hand **bit-banged**, `ds18b20.cpp`, `delayMicroseconds` timing), reads **THREE SHT45s** (air temp/humidity, all I2C 0x44, behind a TCA9548A bus switch at 0x70) on **PA11=SDA/PA12=SCL** off the gated rail (gate = **PA8**, active-low) — but **the SHT45s are no longer on the board: each sits at the far end of its own ≤5 m cable** (ch0 หัวโรงเรือน, ch1 ท้ายโรงเรือน, ch2 นอกโรงเรือน as an ambient reference in a radiation shield), which is why the downstream pull-ups are **2.2k not 4.7k** and why the mux now earns its keep twice over: only the selected branch's ~320 pF loads the bus, so three 5 m branches never add up. **CO2 is a Senseair S88 LP on Modbus RTU/RS-485, NOT I2C** (`src/s88.{h,cpp}`, LPUART1 PC1=TX/PC0=RX, 9600 8N1, **`S88_DE` on PA7** driving a 3.3 V transceiver's tied `DE`+`!RE` — an auto-direction part was the first choice but the common ones (MAX13487E) are **5 V** and their `RO` would drive 5 V into a non-5V-tolerant STM32WL pin; the sensor end drives its own MAX485 from the S88's `UART_R/T` pin. `s88.cpp` drops DE **after `flush()`**, which blocks until the last stop bit is out — a delay there instead would truncate the frame). Measures the **24 V solar bank through a 300k/30k divider on PB3=ADC1_IN2**, scaled by VREFINT-recovered VDDA (`battery.cpp`) — **the old bare-VREFINT battery read is dead**, because the MCU now sits behind a buck and VDDA is a constant 3.3 V, TX's **one uplink** (AS923 **923.2 MHz SF9BW125 CR4/5 14 dBm**), then sleeps. **Uplink-only** (never listens). It can't reach the HTTP web-server directly, so a **36-byte binary frame v5** (`src/lora/lora_packet.h`: magic/nodeId/seq/flags/probe0·probe1 int16 centi-°C/battery_mv/air_temp0·humidity0/pressure/co2/probe2..probe5/air_temp1·humidity1·air_temp2·humidity2/CRC-8) goes to **`lora-gateway/`**. **v4 (2026-08) widened the water temperatures from 2 probes to 6 and is purely additive**: probes 0/1 stay in v1's original two slots with their HOT/COLD flags, so v1/v2/v3 are byte-unchanged and an older two-probe node keeps working and keeps its metric names; probes 2-5 are appended after `co2`. **It adds no valid-flags and cannot** — all eight `flags` bits are spoken for (a v5 needing one must add a second flags byte) — so probes 2-5 signal 'no reading' with the existing `LORA_TEMP_INVALID` (0x8000) sentinel, and `lora_probe_valid()` in the shared header is the ONE place that knows probes 0/1 gate on a flag while 2-5 gate on the sentinel; node, gateway and the Python receiver all call it rather than testing either by hand. A probe that is not fitted needs no config change: an unconnected line sees no presence pulse, goes out as the sentinel, and is dropped by the gateway rather than appearing as a convincing 0.00 degC. **v5 (2026-08) then widened the air sensors from 1 SHT45 to 3**, same additive rule (v1-v4 byte-unchanged, air sensor 0 stays in the v2 slots with its AIR/HUM flags, sensors 1-2 appended after the probes and gated by sentinels — `LORA_TEMP_INVALID` for temperature and the new `LORA_HUM_INVALID` 0xFFFF for humidity, since %RH's valid range is 0..10000). `lora_air_valid()`/`lora_hum_valid()` are the air-side counterparts of `lora_probe_valid()`. Airtime grows ~41 ms per 8 bytes at SF9BW125 (20 B ~185 ms, 28 B ~226 ms, 36 B ~267 ms); the whole v3->v5 growth is ~4 mAs/wake — irrelevant beside the SCD41 — and costs no extra rail-on time at all. The LoRa driver (`src/lora/subghz_lora.c`, called from `main.cpp` via `extern "C"`) is a lean **self-contained SX126x command driver over `HAL_SUBGHZ`** — deliberately **not** the full CubeWL SubGHz_Phy middleware; RF-switch truth table (FE_CTRL1=PC4, FE_CTRL2=PC5, FE_CTRL3=PC3) + TCXO-on-DIO3 taken verbatim from ST's NUCLEO-WL55JC BSP. No `secrets.h` (raw LoRa needs no keys); config in committed `include/node_config.h`. Debug log on `Serial` (LPUART1 → ST-LINK VCP). **Verified: compiles clean** (`pio run`) — RAM 3.1%, Flash 15.4%. **DS18B20 bring-up test (`[env:bluepill_f103c8]`):** while the WL55 boards are on back-order, a second PlatformIO env reuses the **same `ds18b20.cpp`** (via `build_src_filter`, no copy) on an **STM32F103C8T6 Blue Pill** to validate the sensor + wiring — `src/test/ds18b20_test_main.cpp` reads two probes (hot=PB6, cold=PB7, direct 3V3, PC13 LED) and prints over **USB CDC** at ~1 Hz; `ds18b20.h` was made family-agnostic (`#include <Arduino.h>`) so it compiles on both targets. **Hardware-verified on the F103** (both probes track real, independent temperature). The F103 prototype grew several extra envs (all reusing the shipped drivers via `build_src_filter`, none affecting the WL55 build): **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_semihosting]`** — same DS18B20 test but output via **ARM semihosting over SWD** (`-DUSE_SEMIHOSTING`, `BKPT 0xAB`/`SYS_WRITE0`, viewed in the OpenOCD/VSCode Debug Console) for when a clone Blue Pill's native USB won't enumerate; **`-DDS18B20_INTERNAL_PULLUP`** uses the MCU's internal ~40k pull-up when no external 4.7k is on hand (short bench bus only); without it `ds18b20_init()` takes the `GPIO_NOPULL` path for an external resistor. Because that is a property of the **bench wiring**, not of a build, it is set in **one** place — `ds18b20_pullup` in `platformio.ini`'s `[bluepill_base]`, interpolated by every F103 env (the WL55 env never sets it — the real node always has its resistors). **Currently: external 4.7k per line, hardware-verified** reading both probes. A **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_dump]`** diagnostic env (+ `ds18b20_read_scratchpad()`) prints the raw 9-byte scratchpad and probes each DQ pin's idle level (internal pull-up vs pull-down) — `pull-up=1 pull-down=1` proves an external resistor is really on 3V3, `pull-up=0 pull-down=0` means the line is held low (resistor tied to GND / short), all-zero bytes mean a stuck-low bus. Added after a rewire held both DQ lines low. **`tools/watch.ps1`** wraps flash + semihosting-attach into one command (`tools/watch.ps1 [-PioEnv <env>] [-NoUpload]`), resolving pio/openocd under `~/.platformio` and killing a leftover openocd that would otherwise block the ST-Link. Also: `ds18b20_read()` now **rejects an all-zero scratchpad** (stuck-low bus passes CRC → fake 0.00 °C) — a fix that benefits the WL55 node too. **Probe cable runs (design point 5–10 m, was 20 m):** plain 1-Wire handles it (Maxim **AN148**) — **not** RS-485 (a MAX485 *cannot* sit on the 1-Wire line: it's a directional differential UART transceiver, 1-Wire is bidirectional open-drain reversing direction mid-bit-slot). Fit an **external 4.7 kΩ** pull-up — at 10 m of Cat5 the line reaches VIH in ~3 us against the 9 us `ow_read_bit()` allows between release and sample, so 4.7k is sufficient and the board now uses ONE pull-up value; 3.3k only for shielded cable at 10 m, 2.2k only past ~15 m (not the internal ~40 kΩ; drop `DS18B20_INTERNAL_PULLUP`), **twisted-pair DQ+GND**, prefer plain UTP over shielded (roughly half the pF/m), keep 3-wire VDD power; no firmware timing change. Escalate to a **DS2483/DS2480B** 1-Wire line-driver IC (active pull-up), or a remote MCU digitizing + RS-485/Modbus, only past ~30 m. See `water-temp-node/README.md` "Probe cable runs". **Real-hardware design (`water-temp-node/docs/hardware-interface.md`):** the spec for the non-breadboard build, fixed as **two PCBs joined by a CABLE** — the NUCLEO-WL55JC1 (brain) + a **large custom front-end PCB** sitting beside it (the connector decision is spelled out below). Earlier never-built revisions of this spec (a morpho-stacking board, then an ARDUINO shield) have been **deleted from the doc** — if you meet a stale reference to `PB2`, `DQ_HOT`/PA10 or CN5/CN8 pin numbers anywhere, it is dead config, not an alternative. **Eighteen signals** cross the joint (`V3V3_MCU`/2x`GND`/`SENS_GATE` PA8 active-low/`DQ_P0..P5`/`I2C_SDA`/`I2C_SCL`/`S88_TX` PC1/`S88_RX` PC0/`S88_DE` PA7/`VBAT_SENSE` PB3/`DBG_TX` PB6/`DBG_RX` PB7) — **grew 12→18 in 2026-08** and older front-end boards are again not forward-compatible; `VSENS` (the gated rail) is generated on the front-end and never crosses back. **The three SHT45s add ZERO signals**, which is why they are arranged the way they are: the SHT4x I2C address is fixed at the factory by the order code (`SHT45-AD1B` = 0x44, no address pin), so three cannot share a bus — and **individually power-gating them does not work**, which is the trap worth remembering. An unpowered SHT45 still clamps SDA/SCL to its own VDD through its ESD diodes, so the 4.7k bus pull-ups phantom-power it to ~2.7 V (the part runs from 1.08 V) and it answers at 0x44 anyway; no pull-down value escapes it (10k leaves it alive at ~1.8 V, 1k kills it but clamps SDA to ~1.1 V, under the 2.31 V VIH). **What has to be switched is the BUS, not the power** — hence a **TCA9548A** (`src/tca9548a.{h,cpp}`, one channel at a time), which is itself an I2C device and so costs no pins and no connector signals. **Nothing sits upstream of the switch any more** — the SCD41 that used to be at 0x62 was replaced by the S88 LP on RS-485, so an I2C scan with all channels closed should now find **0x70 and nothing else** (the all-channels-closed I2C scan in the bring-up order). Sensor index = mux channel = frame slot = dashboard metric (`I2C_MUX_CHANNELS` in `node_config.h`); three identical 0x44 parts are otherwise indistinguishable, so the channel number is silkscreened next to each. Each downstream channel needs its OWN 4.7k pull-ups (the mux passes signals, not pull-ups). `main.cpp` hides all of it behind one `sht45_select(i)`. Front-end schematic + BOM (4.7k DQ pull-ups **on the switched rail**, 2.2k downstream I2C pull-ups for the 5 m branches, 100R series, low-C TVS, `VSENS` bulk bounded by the 10 ms `DS_POWER_SETTLE_MS`), **the 24 V solar power tree** (F1 fuse → Q2 reverse FET → D9 SMBJ33A TVS → two INDEPENDENT bucks: U6 24→5 V ungated for the S88, U7 24→3.3 V for MCU + gated sensor rail; input spec 18–32 V continuous; U7 must never exceed 3.6 V or it kills CN6-4 and the MCU. **Revision 1.0 (2026-09): U6/U7 are off-the-shelf Traco `TSR 1-2450` / `TSR 1-2433` modules** (36 V in, 1 A, ±2 %, SIP-3 78xx footprint, no external network) — the previous design, two discrete TI `LM5164DDAR` COT bucks with a hand-tuned Type-3 ripple-injection network, ~30 passives and a shared 16 V EN/UVLO divider, was judged too complex for a first board and is preserved verbatim in `docs/hardware-interface-back.md` (+ `.th.md`), banner-marked SUPERSEDED; do not build from it. What the swap traded away, all written down in §5 *The buck modules*: the **≥60 V part rule became 36 V** (D9 still clamps surge energy but at up to 53.3 V, above the module rating; a controller passing panel V_oc ~44 V kills both modules — known v1.0 limitation, mitigated only by a procedural check before step 1 of bring-up), **10.5 µA quiescent became 1 mA typ per module** (energy budget 2.5 → ~3.8 Wh/day, still ~5 % of a 20 W panel), and the **UVLO is gone on purpose** — connect to the battery terminal, not the controller's LOAD output (trap #3), and the node now reports `VBAT_SENSE` down to 6.5 V. **Why not the DFRobot DFR0570/0571 or any MP1584/LM2596 module: they are 28 V parts and a 24 V bank sits at 28.8 V on absorb / 29.2 V for 8S LiFePO4 every afternoon.** Because the 5 V rail is now fixed 5.0 V ±2 % (was 5.1 V), the S88 cable is specified **22 AWG** so the head still sees ≥4.75 V. Per-module `C_IN` is **22 µF/50 V** — Traco's own requirement for V_in > 32 V, not a choice; C15 100 µF on the 5 V rail (total ~222 µF, under the module's 470 µF capacitive-load limit) and C11 100 µF/**63 V** (D9 clamp) are unchanged. All former open items are closed against the TSR 1 datasheet rev. 2026-07-02: pinout **1 = +V_in, 2 = GND, 3 = +V_out**, 1 mA typ no-load (no max published → bring-up step 1 measures it), 250 % current limit, *no PCB traces under the module*; TSR 1-2433 is 4.75–36 V in and in Digi-Key TH stock (10 k, ฿206.49 on 2026-09-02). Earlier corrections retained from the LM5164 revision: C11 **63 V not 50 V**, and Q2 is `DMP6023LE` (60 V), not the 30 V `DMP3056L`. **`Q2` then got its own section too** (*Q2 — the reverse-polarity FET is wired backwards on purpose*): **DMP6023LE-13**, SOT-223, −60 V / 7 A / 28 mΩ @ V_GS=−10 V, **tab = drain**. Two build errors both pass a correct-polarity bench test, which is why they are written down. (a) **Orientation** — a P-FET's body diode runs **drain→source**, so reverse-polarity protection needs **drain to the supply, source to the load**, the opposite of an ideal-diode/ORing hookup; wired the other way it conducts happily when reversed and protects nothing. (b) **The gate clamp is mandatory** — gate pulled to GND puts V_GS at −V_bank, i.e. −32 V at the top of the design range against a **±20 V** absolute maximum, so `R38` 470 k + `D10` 12 V Zener gate-to-source are a pair, not an option. Also settled there: why the chain is **F1 → Q2 → D9** (a TVS ahead of the FET would be forward-biased by a reversed connection — putting it after Q2 is what lets D9 be a *unidirectional* SMBJ33A), and that **F1 must be specified by I²t, not amps** — hot-plug inrush runs through Q2's *body diode*, not the 1.2 ms gate ramp, at ~0.1 A²s, which some 1206 "slow-blow" 2 A parts sit right on; use a 5×20 mm T2A cartridge), and a bring-up order that now starts with power. **`SENS_GATE` changed purpose**: on solar its 5 µA leakage budget is irrelevant, so it is kept purely as the **I2C/1-Wire fault-recovery mechanism** (three unshielded 5 m branches in a greenhouse will latch SDA low eventually — gate off, wait, gate on, retry). The S88 is **not** behind it. **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_sx1278]`** — bench self-test of an **external SX1278 (SX127x) LoRa module** (`src/sx1278.{h,cpp}`, Arduino SPI; a *different* chip/registers from the WL55's internal SX126x): SPI probe (RegVersion==0x12) → 433 MHz SF9/BW125 TX beacon; **hardware-verified** (RegVersion OK + TxDone). **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_node]`** — the full prototype **node**: DS18B20 → the shared **12-byte `lora_packet.h`** frame → SX1278 TX @433 (NSS=PA4 SCK=PA5 MISO=PA6 MOSI=PA7 RESET=PB0 DIO0=PB1); **hardware-verified** (reads probes + TxDone). This F103+SX1278 node is the 433 MHz prototype that pairs with **`lora-pi-receiver/`** (SX1278 = 433 MHz, so it does **not** reach the 923 MHz WL55 gateway — separate prototype path while the WL55 boards are on back-order). **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_node_standalone]`** is the same node **without** semihosting so it runs autonomously on any USB/battery power (the semihosting `BKPT` faults with no debugger attached; `out()` falls back to a harmless UART `Serial`) — flash it once and just power the board. **End-to-end HARDWARE-VERIFIED (2026-08):** F103 node → SX1278 433 → Pi SX1278 (SPI) → `lora-pi-receiver/receiver.py` → web-server accepted (HTTP 2xx), dashboard `water-temp-01` `temp_hot`/`temp_cold` matched the node exactly at RSSI ≈ −64 dBm / SNR ≈ 13 dB. **BME280 added (2026-08):** the node envs also read a **BME280 on I²C2 (PB10=SCL/PB11=SDA — I²C1/PB6-7 is the DS18B20 bus)** via the Adafruit BME280 lib and send **ambient air_temp/humidity/pressure** in an **18-byte frame v2** (magic `0xA2`). `lora_packet.h` gained v2 **additively** (`lora_packet_pack_v2`, new flags/fields) — **v1/12-byte is unchanged so the WL55 923 MHz path is untouched**; only the F103+BME280 433 path sends v2. `lora-pi-receiver/lora_packet.py` decodes **both** v1 and v2; `receiver.py` adds `air_temp`/`humidity`/`pressure` metrics. **SHT45 + SCD41 added (2026-08):** the node gained two more I²C sensors — a **SHT45** (air temp/humidity, 0x44) and a **Sensirion SCD41** (true photoacoustic NDIR **CO2**, 0x62) — on **both** the WL55 node and the F103 prototype, in a **20-byte frame v3** (magic `0xA3`) that appends `co2` to v2's layout; **v1/v2 are byte-unchanged**, and `lora_packet_unpack()` now accepts all three so an older node keeps working. Drivers are **hand-rolled from the datasheets** (`src/sht45.{cpp,h}`, `src/scd41.{cpp,h}`) over a shared `src/sensirion_i2c.{cpp,h}` helper (Sensirion word protocol: 16-bit BE words each followed by **CRC-8 poly 0x31/init 0xFF** — deliberately *not* the frame's `lora_crc8`, poly 0x07/init 0x00) — no vendor library, so the WL55 env still has **zero `lib_deps`**. **SHT45 supersedes the BME280 for air temp/humidity** (±0.1 °C/±1 %RH beats it); the BME280 keeps `pressure` only, and `LORA_FLAG_SHT` (bit 7) records which part supplied T/RH — same units either way, so it is *not* emitted as a metric (dashboard history stays continuous) but is logged once per node by `receiver.py`, and again only on change. **CO2 pacing is GONE on the WL55 (2026-08).** The F103 prototype still runs its SCD41 periodic with BME280 pressure compensation, but the WL55's S88 LP **runs continuously** and `CO2_ENABLED`/`CO2_EVERY_N_WAKES`/`CO2_SINGLE_SHOT_WARMUP` are deleted. The reason is the S88's **ABC (Automatic Baseline Correction): an 8-day period, with accuracy specified at *continuous* operation** — at the old 0.28 % duty cycle, eight days of *powered* time would take **7.8 years**, so ABC would never complete once and CO2 drift would go permanently uncorrected. On 24 V solar the S88's 18 mA average is **90 mW ≈ 2.2 Wh/day, ~3 % of a 20 W panel's ~80 Wh/day**, so continuous operation is simply affordable. Unplanned bonus: 90 mW of self-heating holds the module a degree or two above ambient, which keeps local RH off the dew point — duty-cycling it in a greenhouse would have made condensation **worse**. The cache in `main.cpp` survives, for a different job: riding out a failed Modbus exchange rather than dropping the metric. WL55 pins: **I2C2 PA11=SDA/PA12=SCL** (I2C1 wants PA9/PA10 = the probes; I2C3 wants PB11 = the Nucleo's **LED3**), on the gated VSENS rail — `sensor_pins_park()` parks the I2C pins analog too. **`I2C_POWER_SETTLE_MS` dropped 1000 ms → 10 ms** when the SCD41 left: that 1 s was the SCD41's power-up time and had to be overlapped with the 750 ms conversion to avoid paying it twice; the SHT45 needs ~1 ms, so both settle windows now collapse to the same 10 ms and rail-on time is set purely by `DS_CONVERT_MS`. **UART pin hunt (the constraint that shaped this revision):** the only ADC-capable pin left on CN10 is **PB3** (PA15/PB1/PB2/PB4/PB13/PB14 are not on CN10 at all; PA11/PA12 are the I2C pair; PA13/PA14 are SWD), and the only free UART TX was PC1 — USART2 exists **only** on PA2/PA3, and PA2/PA3 are **not on the morpho at all**, so the ST-LINK VCP can never be reached from the front-end. Hence LPUART1 (PC1/PC0) for the S88, **USART1 (PB6/PB7, CN10-35/37) out to a 3-pin debug header J13** for a USB-serial adapter, and CN10-35/37 leaving the do-not-route list — they were ⛔ only because they were *assumed* to be the VCP. `docs/hardware-interface.md` updated: the connector contract grew **six → eight** (+SDA/SCL) → **twelve** (+four probe lines) → **eighteen** (+S88 UART/DE, +VBAT_SENSE, +debug UART) — **older front-end boards are not forward-compatible at any of these steps**. The SCD41's 205 mA peaks no longer size Q1 (the S88's 300 mA peaks are on their own ungated 5.1 V rail, so `VSENS` now carries only ~9 mA of probes plus µA of SHT45s), and **the front-end enclosure no longer needs venting** — nothing in it measures air any more, so it should be IP65 and as sealed as the glands allow. The venting requirement moved out to the sensor heads, where the S88 needs a ventilated radiation shield with the **diffusion area facing downward** and a PTFE/Gore membrane. **Eleven cable entries** now set the box size: 6 probes + 3 SHT45 branches + 1 S88 head + 1 solar input. **The front-end is now a LARGE CUSTOM PCB joined by a CABLE, not a shield and not a morpho-stacking board** (switched 2026-08, after the ARDUINO-shield revision it replaces): six probe connectors + the two I2C parts + protection + battery input do not fit a 68.6×53.4 mm Uno outline, and once the board must be its own size the shield's only advantage (free keying from the outline) is gone anyway. **Two cables, deliberately split:** all **12** logic signals ride ONE 2×19 IDC ribbon on **morpho CN10** (`SENS_GATE`(**PA8**)→CN10-16, `DQ_P0..P5`→CN10-11/17/19/21/23/25, `I2C_SDA`(PA11)→CN10-5, `I2C_SCL`(PA12)→CN10-3, `GND`→CN10-9 **and** CN10-20), while battery power takes its **own keyed 2-pin lead** to **CN6-4/6/7**. Keeping `VBAT` off the ribbon is the point: a 2×19 socket on unshrouded morpho pins *can* be pressed on 180°-rotated, and with power split out the worst case is two open-drain GPIOs shorted to ground instead of battery voltage on a GPIO. **Keying is a physical stop, not a silkscreen arrow:** clip the **CN10-6** pin (it is NC) and plug the matching IDC hole — rotated, that hole lands on populated pin 33 (PB12) and the socket will not seat. **Use full-length sockets** (2×19 over all of CN10, 1×8 over all of CN6 with only 4/6/7 wired) so neither can be fitted one position out. Ribbon ≤30 cm. **Probe pins are placed by ribbon adjacency, not convenience** — six bit-banged open-drain lines in one flat cable is the new SI problem, so each DQ sits next to a **GND (CN10-9/20)** or an **NC (6/10/18/24/34, groundable at the front-end end only, as a one-ended guard)**; every other conductor floats at the front-end. **`SENS_GATE` is PA8** (CN10-16, inside the DQ block, no boot strap so it resets floating and the 100k holds the rail off), and **PA10 is deliberately unused** — a fine 1-Wire pin, but on CN7, and one line does not justify a second ribbon. **The do-not-route list is real again** — cabling to the morpho puts PC3/PC4/PC5 (RF switch), PB0 (TCXO), PA13/PA14 (SWD), the VCP, AVDD/AGND, all three buttons (PA0/PA1/PC6) and all three LEDs (PB9/PB11/PB15) back in reach — but SWD *can* now be brought out to a debug header, which the shield could not. Caveat recorded in the doc: SCL at CN10-3 sits between the RF-switch conductors CN10-2/4, tolerable only because the firmware gates the sensor rail off **before** powering the radio, so I2C is never active while those switch — do not reorder that sequence in `main.cpp`. The verified **morpho CN7 map** is retained as **Appendix A** for bench probing (CN10 is now in the main text), including that **CN7-33 `VBAT` is the RTC backup pin, NOT a power input** (a trap the shield could not reach and this revision can), and that **Table 18 has an erratum** — CN10-37 is printed `PB6 / PA3` but is `PB7 / PA3` (PB6 is already at pin 35, PB7 appears nowhere in a table whose preamble says all I/Os are present, and the UM's own note says D0/D1 = PB6 **and** PB7).
