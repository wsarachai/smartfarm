# water-temp-node/

- `water-temp-node/` — **PlatformIO / Arduino (STM32duino)** firmware for a **NUCLEO-WL55JC1** (STM32WL55JC, `board = nucleo_wl55jc`, `framework = arduino`, flash via onboard/external **ST-Link**). A **solar-powered LoRa** multi-probe **water-temperature** (up to 6) + **air/CO2** sensor node — the repo's **first non-ESP, first LoRa, first sub-GHz** device. The deployment is a **mushroom house** (โรงเรือนเพาะเห็ด): dark, misted, 90–95 %RH, 15–30 °C, and CO2-rich by design. That building drives most of the design decisions below, and text written for a *plant* greenhouse (sun loads, radiation shields, CO2 drawn *down* by photosynthesis) is wrong here in ways that are easy to miss.

  **Why `arduino` not `stm32cube`:** PlatformIO's ststm32 platform has **no** stm32cube framework for the STM32WL family (no `framework-stm32cubewl` package exists in the official *or* maxgerhardt platforms; the WL board advertises only `arduino`/`zephyr`). But **STM32duino *is* the STM32Cube HAL underneath**, so the intended HAL SubGHz driver compiles unchanged — we just enable `HAL_SUBGHZ` via `include/hal_conf_extra.h` (the STM32duino override hook).

  **The wake cycle.** Every `WAKE_INTERVAL_S` (default 15 min) it wakes from **Stop2** (raw-HAL `HAL_PWREx_EnterSTOP2Mode` + RTC/LSE wake on the CM4 `RTC_WKUP_IRQn` — **not** the STM32LowPower lib, whose 2.0.0 release `#error`s against the core PlatformIO bundles), powers the whole sensor front-end through one **AO3401A P-MOSFET** high-side gate (PA8, active-low; 100k gate pull-up = OFF while Hi-Z), reads everything, sends **one** LoRa uplink, and sleeps. **Uplink-only** (never listens).

  **EVERYTHING the front-end reads is behind that one gate, including CO2.** There is no always-on sensor rail on this board, which is what makes `SENS_GATE` a complete fault-recovery mechanism rather than a partial one (three unshielded 5 m branches in a wet building *will* latch SDA low eventually — gate off, wait, gate on, retry). This was **not** true before revision 2.0, when the CO2 part needed continuous 5 V; a board with an ungated sensor rail is not this design.

  **Sensors.** **6× DS18B20** (one per GPIO, SKIP-ROM, so they convert in PARALLEL — one 750 ms wait, not six; P0=PA5 P1=PA4 P2=PA9 P3=PC2 P4=**PB8** P5=PB10; 12-bit, hand **bit-banged**, `ds18b20.cpp`, `delayMicroseconds` timing). **P4 moved off PC1 in 2026-08** to free it for a CO2 sensor on RS-485; that subsystem was deleted in 2026-09 and **PC1/PC0/PA7 are now free and deliberately unused**, but P4 **stays** on PB8 — moving it back would churn the connector contract, the layout and the pin map for no gain. **THREE SHT45s** (air temp/humidity, all I2C 0x44) and **one Sensirion SCD41** (photoacoustic NDIR **CO2**, 0x62) sit behind a **TCA9548A** bus switch at 0x70 on **I2C2 = PA11(SDA)/PA12(SCL)**, SHT45s on channels 0–2 and **SCD41 on channel 3**. I2C1 wants PA9/PA10 (PA9 is a probe); I2C3 wants PB11 (the Nucleo's LED3). `sensor_pins_park()` parks the I2C pins analog in sleep.

  **None of the I2C sensors are on the board** — each sits at the far end of its own ≤5 m cable (ch0 หัวโรงเรือน, ch1 ท้ายโรงเรือน, ch2 นอกโรงเรือน as an outdoor reference, ch3 CO2 กลางโรงเรือน). Hence the **downstream pull-ups are 2.2k, not 4.7k** (R17–R22 for the SHT45s, R39/R40 for CO2): 4.7k over 5 m gives a 1.27 µs rise against a 1000 ns budget, so it passes on a bench lead and NACKs intermittently with the real cable — the nastiest fault on the board. Upstream R15/R16 stay 4.7k (30 mm of trace). The mux also earns its keep twice over: only the selected branch's ~320 pF loads the bus, so four 5 m branches never add up. Sensor index = mux channel = frame slot = dashboard metric (`I2C_MUX_CHANNELS`); identical 0x44 parts are otherwise indistinguishable, so silkscreen the channel next to each. **Nothing sits upstream of the switch** — an all-channels-closed I2C scan must find **0x70 and nothing else**, no 0x44 and no 0x62.

  **Why the SHT45s cannot simply be power-gated individually** (the trap worth remembering): the SHT4x address is fixed at the factory by order code (`SHT45-AD1B` = 0x44, no address pin), so three cannot share a bus — and an *unpowered* SHT45 still clamps SDA/SCL to its own VDD through its ESD diodes, so the bus pull-ups phantom-power it to ~2.7 V (the part runs from 1.08 V) and it answers anyway. No pull-down value escapes it (10k leaves it alive at ~1.8 V; 1k kills it but clamps SDA to ~1.1 V, under the 2.31 V VIH). **What has to be switched is the BUS, not the power** — hence the TCA9548A, which is itself an I2C device and so costs no pins and no connector signals. **The three SHT45s add ZERO signals to the joint.**

  **CO2 — Sensirion SCD41** (`src/scd41.{h,cpp}` + `src/sensirion_i2c.cpp`, hand-rolled, hardware-verified on the F103). **Revision 2.0 (2026-09) replaced a Senseair S88 LP** that had held the slot since 2026-08 and took the entire RS-485 subsystem with it: U4, U5, the head LDO, the A/B TVS pairs, **the whole 5 V rail and its Traco TSR 1-2450**, and three connector signals — so the joint went **18 → 15 signals**. **Why:** the S88 LP is an **0–85 %RH** part (derating to 45 % at 50 °C) and this building runs at 90–95 %RH. The SCD41 is **−10…60 °C / 0–95 %RH**, **2.4–5.5 V** so it runs off the same 3.3 V as everything else, and speaks **I2C with a CRC-8 on every 16-bit word**, so dropping Modbus costs no data integrity. The full S88 design is preserved in `docs/hardware-interface-s88.md` (+ `.th.md`) and is *not* live config — if you meet `S88_*` symbols, `s88.{h,cpp}`, HR27 pressure or ABC-vs-400-ppm reasoning anywhere outside that archive, it is stale.

  **The two SCD41 things most likely to be "optimised" wrongly:**
  - **ASC ships ENABLED and is actively harmful here.** It assumes weekly exposure to 400 ppm outdoor air and calibrates the lowest reading it has seen toward that. A mushroom house never reaches 400 ppm (a ventilated fruiting room sits at 800–1500), so ASC would drag every reading DOWN → the controller believes the air is fine → the house is **under-ventilated** → long stems, small caps, **and nothing looks wrong on the dashboard.** Doubly determined: ASC is also **not available at all** in power-cycled single-shot operation (datasheet §3.11). `scd41_ensure_asc(0)` enforces it OFF on **every** wake (1 ms read, EEPROM written only on mismatch, `persist_settings` 0x3615) — because a *replacement* sensor arrives with ASC on. Recalibration is manual **FRC** (0x362f) in outdoor air at each crop changeover, after a run-up of **5 minutes at a 1-minute sampling period**; it returns 0xFFFF and does nothing if the sensor has not been measuring.
  - **The throwaway first shot.** `scd41_read_single_shot(warmup=1)` takes two 5 s shots and discards the first — **~10 s of sensor-on per wake**, taking the gated window to ~11 s. **The current datasheet does not say to do this.** Datasheet v1.3–v1.6 did ("After a power cycle, the initial single shot reading should be discarded"); **v1.7 removed it** ("removed recommendation to discard initial single shot measurement after power cycle"); the *SCD4x Low Power Operation* app note (v1.0, Jul 2022) §2.4 still says it, and harder. Sensirion never said why it went, and that app note is stale elsewhere (its flowcharts keep the 1000 ms power-up that datasheet **v1.4 corrected to 30 ms**). We keep the shot on an asymmetric-cost argument, not a bibliographic one: unnecessary it wastes **0.007 Wh/day** out of ~80; necessary and skipped, the CO2 reads low and the failure is the invisible one above. Don't delete it without a positive statement from Sensirion. The reasoning is written out in `src/scd41.h` and §3 of the hardware spec.

  Altitude uses **`set_sensor_altitude` (EEPROM)**, not `set_ambient_pressure` (RAM, lost every wake) — `SCD41_SITE_ALTITUDE_M` = 330 for Maejo, set **before** any FRC. Range: output 0–40 000 ppm, specified 400–5000 — fine for fruiting-phase ventilation control (400–3000) and on-scale but out-of-spec during spawn run; if calibrated spawn-run figures are ever needed the documented fallback is the **Senseair S88 GH** (004-1-0102, 0–20 000 ppm, 0–95 %RH), which brings 5 V and RS-485 back. Parts: **`SEK-SCD41-SENSOR` breakout recommended** (the bare `SCD41-D-R2` is reflow-only LGA, MSL 1, ≤245 °C, no vapour phase, no board wash, and full CO2 accuracy returns only ~5 days after reflow — so never FRC a freshly built head), both Digi-Key TH.

  **205 mA peak is the CO2 sensor's real engineering cost** (175 typ / 205 max, ~10× anything else on the rail; its *average* is negligible). Handled three ways: **22 AWG on the CO2 branch** (0.53 Ω round trip → **109 mV** IR drop, vs 173 mV at 24 AWG), **100 µF low-ESR + 100 nF at the head**, and `main.cpp` reading the SCD41 **LAST inside the gated window**, after every DS18B20 conversion and all three SHT45s, so its bursts never overlap anything that cares. The head capacitor does a **second** job worth knowing about: the SCD41 asks for ≤**30 mV** supply ripple and recommends a dedicated LDO, while U7 is a switcher at **75 mV p-p typ** — the board fails that at the regulator and passes it at the sensor, because the 5 m cable's inductance plus the head capacitor attenuate ~1:40 at 500 kHz (~2 mV). So 22 AWG is load-bearing twice: low resistance for the IR drop, enough resistance to damp the filter's LC (Q ≈ 0.35). That analysis rests on an *estimated* cable inductance, so bring-up **measures** it (§7 step 22).

  **The one axis where the SCD41 is worse than the part it replaced:** it dissipates ~1.5 mW where the S88 gave 90 mW, so the accidental anti-condensation self-heating is gone — in a wetter building. A **~220 Ω heater pad at the head is laid out and left DNP**, to be fitted only if the SHT45 dew-point logs demand it (+1.2 Wh/day). The CO2 head needs a vented, splash-protected housing with the **diffusion opening facing down**, a PTFE/Gore membrane, **never sealed**, and **not in a misting nozzle's path** — but **no radiation shield**, because a mushroom house is dark. (Only the *outdoor* SHT45, ch2, needs one.)

  **Energy: ~0.8 Wh/day total** against ~80 Wh/day from a 20 W panel — **~1 %**. Read the shape, not the total: **72 % is the Traco module's own no-load current**, and the CO2 sensor is **under 2 %** (0.0136 Wh/day, from the app note's 154 mC per useful shot at 3.3 V). There is no load left here worth optimising.

  **Power — 24 V solar.** Measures the bank through a 300k/30k divider on **PB3 = ADC1_IN2**, scaled by VREFINT-recovered VDDA (`battery.cpp`) — **the old bare-VREFINT battery read is dead**, because the MCU now sits behind a buck and VDDA is a constant 3.3 V. Tree: F1 fuse → **Q2** reverse FET → **D9** SMBJ33A TVS → **U7**, a single off-the-shelf **Traco `TSR 1-2433`** module (36 V in, 1 A, ±2 %, SIP-3 78xx footprint, no external network) making the one 3.3 V rail for MCU + gated sensors. Input spec 18–32 V continuous; U7 must never exceed 3.6 V or it kills CN6-4 and the MCU. **Revision 1.0 (2026-09)** replaced two discrete TI `LM5164DDAR` COT bucks (hand-tuned Type-3 ripple-injection network, ~30 passives, shared 16 V EN/UVLO divider) with Traco modules as too complex for a first board — preserved verbatim in `docs/hardware-interface-back.md` (+ `.th.md`), banner-marked SUPERSEDED, and now **two** revisions behind since rev 2.0 also deleted the second module. What the module swap traded away, all in §5: the **≥60 V part rule became 36 V** (D9 still clamps surge but at up to 53.3 V, above the module rating; a controller passing panel V_oc ~44 V kills it — known limitation, mitigated only by a procedural check before bring-up step 1), **10.5 µA quiescent became 1 mA typ** (which is why one regulator is now 72 % of the budget), and the **UVLO is gone on purpose** — connect to the **battery terminal, not the controller's LOAD output** (trap #3), and the node reports `VBAT_SENSE` down to 6.5 V. **Why not DFRobot DFR0570/0571 or any MP1584/LM2596 module: they are 28 V parts, and a 24 V bank sits at 28.8 V on absorb / 29.2 V for 8S LiFePO4 every afternoon.** `C_IN` is **22 µF/50 V** — Traco's own requirement for V_in > 32 V, not a choice; C11 is 100 µF/**63 V** (D9 clamps to 53.3 V, so 50 V is not enough). All open items closed against the TSR 1 datasheet rev. 2026-07-02: pinout **1 = +V_in, 2 = GND, 3 = +V_out**, 1 mA typ no-load (no max published → bring-up step 1 measures it), **250 %** current limit, 470 µF capacitive-load limit, *no PCB traces under the module*; TSR 1-2433 is 4.75–36 V in, Digi-Key TH stock (฿206.49 on 2026-09-02). Retained from the LM5164 revision: **Q2 is `DMP6023LE` (60 V), not the 30 V `DMP3056L`**.

  **`Q2` has its own section** (*Q2 — the reverse-polarity FET is wired backwards on purpose*): **DMP6023LE-13**, SOT-223, −60 V / 7 A / 28 mΩ @ V_GS=−10 V, **tab = drain**. Two build errors both pass a correct-polarity bench test, which is why they are written down. (a) **Orientation** — a P-FET's body diode runs **drain→source**, so reverse-polarity protection needs **drain to the supply, source to the load**, the opposite of an ideal-diode/ORing hookup; wired the other way it conducts happily when reversed and protects nothing. (b) **The gate clamp is mandatory** — gate pulled to GND puts V_GS at −V_bank, i.e. −32 V against a **±20 V** absolute maximum, so `R38` 470 k + `D10` 12 V Zener gate-to-source are a pair, not an option. Also settled there: why the chain is **F1 → Q2 → D9** (a TVS ahead of the FET would be forward-biased by a reversed connection — putting it after Q2 is what lets D9 be a *unidirectional* SMBJ33A), and that **F1 must be specified by I²t, not amps** — hot-plug inrush runs through Q2's *body diode*, not the 1.2 ms gate ramp, at ~0.1 A²s, which some 1206 "slow-blow" 2 A parts sit right on; use a 5×20 mm T2A cartridge.

  **The board-to-board joint.** Real-hardware design lives in `docs/hardware-interface.md` (Thai mirror `.th.md`; **the English file is the source of truth**). Fixed as **two PCBs joined by a CABLE**: the NUCLEO-WL55JC1 (brain) + a **large custom front-end PCB** beside it. Earlier never-built revisions (a morpho-stacking board, then an ARDUINO shield) are **deleted from the doc** — a stale reference to `PB2`, `DQ_HOT`/PA10 or CN5/CN8 pin numbers is dead config, not an alternative. **Fifteen signals** cross the joint: `V3V3_MCU` / 2× `GND` / `SENS_GATE` PA8 active-low / `DQ_P0..P5` / `I2C_SDA` PA11 / `I2C_SCL` PA12 / `VBAT_SENSE` PB3 / `DBG_TX` PB6 / `DBG_RX` PB7. The contract went **six → eight** (+SDA/SCL) → **twelve** (+four probe lines) → **eighteen** (+CO2 UART/DE, +VBAT_SENSE, +debug UART) → **fifteen** in 2026-09. **Older front-end boards are not forward-compatible at any of these steps**, and an eighteen-signal board is not merely over-provisioned for rev 2.0 — it is wrong, because it has a 5 V rail this design does not generate. `VSENS` (the gated rail) is generated on the front-end and never crosses back.

  **Two cables, deliberately split:** logic rides ONE 2×19 IDC ribbon on **morpho CN10** (`SENS_GATE`→CN10-16, `DQ_P0..P5`→CN10-11/17/19/21/23/25, `I2C_SDA`→CN10-5, `I2C_SCL`→CN10-3, `GND`→CN10-9 **and** CN10-20, `VBAT_SENSE`→PB3, debug UART→CN10-35/37), while 3.3 V power takes its **own keyed 2-pin lead** to **CN6-4/6/7**. Keeping power off the ribbon is the point: a 2×19 socket on unshrouded morpho pins *can* be pressed on 180°-rotated, and with power split out the worst case is two open-drain GPIOs shorted to ground instead of supply voltage on a GPIO. **Keying is a physical stop, not a silkscreen arrow:** clip the **CN10-6** pin (it is NC) and plug the matching IDC hole — rotated, that hole lands on populated pin 33 (PB12) and the socket will not seat. **Use full-length sockets** so neither can be fitted one position out. Ribbon ≤30 cm. **Probe pins are placed by ribbon adjacency, not convenience** — six bit-banged open-drain lines in one flat cable is the SI problem, so each DQ sits next to a **GND (CN10-9/20)** or an **NC (6/10/18/24/34, groundable at the front-end end only, as a one-ended guard)**. **`SENS_GATE` is PA8** (CN10-16, inside the DQ block, no boot strap so it resets floating and the 100k holds the rail off); **PA10 is deliberately unused** — a fine 1-Wire pin, but on CN7, and one line does not justify a second ribbon.

  **The do-not-route list is real** — cabling to the morpho puts PC3/PC4/PC5 (RF switch), PB0 (TCXO), PA13/PA14 (SWD), AVDD/AGND, all three buttons (PA0/PA1/PC6) and all three LEDs (PB9/PB11/PB15) back in reach. Caveat recorded in the doc: SCL at CN10-3 sits between RF-switch conductors CN10-2/4, tolerable **only** because the firmware gates the sensor rail off *before* powering the radio, so I2C is never active while those switch — **do not reorder that sequence in `main.cpp`.** **UART pin hunt (the constraint that shaped the connector):** the only ADC-capable pin left on CN10 is **PB3**, and **USART2 exists only on PA2/PA3, which are not on the morpho at all** — so the ST-LINK VCP can never be reached from the front-end. Hence **USART1 (PB6/PB7, CN10-35/37) out to a 3-pin debug header J13** for a USB-serial adapter; CN10-35/37 left the do-not-route list once it was clear they were ⛔ only because they were *assumed* to be the VCP. The verified **morpho CN7 map** is Appendix A for bench probing, including that **CN7-33 `VBAT` is the RTC backup pin, NOT a power input**, and that **UM2592 Table 18 has an erratum** — CN10-37 is printed `PB6 / PA3` but is `PB7 / PA3`. Also flagged: Table 17 labels PA11/PA12 `I2C1_*` while STM32duino's `PeripheralPins.c` and Zephyr both say **I2C2** — harmless for the firmware (STM32duino resolves the instance from the pin map) but check RM0453 before any bare-HAL port.

  **Enclosure:** the front-end box **no longer needs venting** — nothing inside it measures air — so IP65 and as sealed as the glands allow. Venting moved out to the sensor heads. **Eleven cable entries** set the box size: 6 probes + 3 SHT45 branches + 1 CO2 head + 1 solar input.

  **The LoRa link.** TX is **one uplink** per wake (AS923 **923.2 MHz SF9BW125 CR4/5 14 dBm**). It can't reach the HTTP web-server directly, so a **36-byte binary frame v5** (`src/lora/lora_packet.h`: magic/nodeId/seq/flags/probe0·probe1 int16 centi-°C/battery_mv/air_temp0·humidity0/pressure/co2/probe2..probe5/air_temp1·humidity1·air_temp2·humidity2/CRC-8) goes to **`lora-gateway/`**. **v4 (2026-08) widened water temperatures from 2 probes to 6 and is purely additive**: probes 0/1 stay in v1's original two slots with their HOT/COLD flags, so v1/v2/v3 are byte-unchanged and an older two-probe node keeps working with its metric names; probes 2–5 are appended after `co2`. **It adds no valid-flags and cannot** — all eight `flags` bits are spoken for (a future version needing one must add a second flags byte) — so probes 2–5 signal 'no reading' with the existing `LORA_TEMP_INVALID` (0x8000) sentinel, and **`lora_probe_valid()` in the shared header is the ONE place** that knows probes 0/1 gate on a flag while 2–5 gate on the sentinel; node, gateway and the Python receiver all call it rather than testing either by hand. A probe that is not fitted needs no config change: an unconnected line sees no presence pulse, goes out as the sentinel, and is dropped by the gateway rather than appearing as a convincing 0.00 °C. **v5 (2026-08) widened the air sensors from 1 SHT45 to 3**, same additive rule (air sensor 0 stays in the v2 slots with its AIR/HUM flags; sensors 1–2 appended and gated by sentinels — `LORA_TEMP_INVALID` for temperature and `LORA_HUM_INVALID` 0xFFFF for humidity, since %RH's valid range is 0..10000). `lora_air_valid()`/`lora_hum_valid()` are the air-side counterparts. Airtime grows ~41 ms per 8 bytes at SF9BW125 (20 B ~185 ms, 28 B ~226 ms, 36 B ~267 ms); the whole v3→v5 growth is ~4 mAs/wake and costs no extra rail-on time at all.

  The LoRa driver (`src/lora/subghz_lora.c`, called from `main.cpp` via `extern "C"`) is a lean **self-contained SX126x command driver over `HAL_SUBGHZ`** — deliberately **not** the full CubeWL SubGHz_Phy middleware; RF-switch truth table (FE_CTRL1=PC4, FE_CTRL2=PC5, FE_CTRL3=PC3) + TCXO-on-DIO3 taken verbatim from ST's NUCLEO-WL55JC BSP. No `secrets.h` (raw LoRa needs no keys); config in committed `include/node_config.h`. Debug log on USART1 → the 3-pin header, **not** the ST-LINK VCP (feeding 3V3 at CN6-4 leaves the ST-LINK unpowered).

  **Sensirion drivers are hand-rolled from the datasheets** (`src/sht45.{cpp,h}`, `src/scd41.{cpp,h}`) over a shared `src/sensirion_i2c.{cpp,h}` helper (16-bit BE words each followed by **CRC-8 poly 0x31 / init 0xFF** — deliberately *not* the frame's `lora_crc8`, poly 0x07 / init 0x00; the codebase carries two distinct CRCs and mixing them is a silent-corruption bug). No vendor library, so the WL55 env has **zero `lib_deps`**. **SHT45 supersedes the BME280 for air temp/humidity** (±0.1 °C / ±1 %RH beats it); the BME280 survives on the F103 prototype for `pressure` only, and `LORA_FLAG_SHT` (bit 7) records which part supplied T/RH — same units either way, so it is *not* emitted as a metric (dashboard history stays continuous) but logged once per node by `receiver.py`, and again only on change. **The SCD41 also reports its own T/RH and we deliberately throw them away**: the part self-heats, so they read high, and the SHT45 nearby is both more accurate and unheated. They are exposed only because `scd41_read()` gets them free and they are useful in bring-up.

  **`I2C_POWER_SETTLE_MS` is 10 ms.** It was 1000 ms, on the strength of an SCD41 power-up figure Sensirion has since **corrected** (v1.3 really did say 1000 ms; **v1.4 corrected it to 30 ms** and v1.7 still does). The sequencing built around overlapping that window with the 750 ms conversion survives anyway, because it is the right shape and now costs nothing: the SCD41 is addressed last, by which time the rail has been up for the whole conversion plus three SHT45 reads.

  **`SENS_GATE` changed purpose** on solar: its 5 µA leakage budget is irrelevant, so it is kept purely as the **I2C/1-Wire fault-recovery mechanism** described above.

  **Verified: all 7 envs build clean** (`pio run`) — WL55 **RAM 3.1 % / Flash 15.7 %**; F103 node 52.9 %, standalone 55.0 %, sx1278 25.4 %.

  **DS18B20 bring-up test (`[env:bluepill_f103c8]`):** a second PlatformIO env reuses the **same `ds18b20.cpp`** (via `build_src_filter`, no copy) on an **STM32F103C8T6 Blue Pill** to validate sensor + wiring — `src/test/ds18b20_test_main.cpp` reads two probes (hot=PB6, cold=PB7, direct 3V3, PC13 LED) and prints over **USB CDC** at ~1 Hz; `ds18b20.h` was made family-agnostic (`#include <Arduino.h>`) so it compiles on both targets. **Hardware-verified on the F103** (both probes track real, independent temperature). Extra F103 envs (all reusing shipped drivers via `build_src_filter`, none affecting the WL55 build): **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_semihosting]`** — same test but output via **ARM semihosting over SWD** (`-DUSE_SEMIHOSTING`, `BKPT 0xAB`/`SYS_WRITE0`, viewed in the OpenOCD/VSCode Debug Console) for when a clone Blue Pill's native USB won't enumerate. **`-DDS18B20_INTERNAL_PULLUP`** uses the MCU's internal ~40k pull-up when no external 4.7k is on hand (short bench bus only); without it `ds18b20_init()` takes the `GPIO_NOPULL` path. Because that is a property of the **bench wiring**, not of a build, it is set in **one** place — `ds18b20_pullup` in `platformio.ini`'s `[bluepill_base]`, interpolated by every F103 env (the WL55 env never sets it — the real node always has its resistors). **Currently: external 4.7k per line, hardware-verified.** A **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_dump]`** diagnostic env (+ `ds18b20_read_scratchpad()`) prints the raw 9-byte scratchpad and probes each DQ pin's idle level — `pull-up=1 pull-down=1` proves an external resistor is really on 3V3, `pull-up=0 pull-down=0` means the line is held low (resistor tied to GND / short), all-zero bytes mean a stuck-low bus. Added after a rewire held both DQ lines low. **`tools/watch.ps1`** wraps flash + semihosting-attach into one command (`tools/watch.ps1 [-PioEnv <env>] [-NoUpload]`), resolving pio/openocd under `~/.platformio` and killing a leftover openocd that would otherwise block the ST-Link. Also: `ds18b20_read()` **rejects an all-zero scratchpad** (a stuck-low bus passes CRC → fake 0.00 °C) — a fix that benefits the WL55 node too.

  **The 433 MHz prototype path.** **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_sx1278]`** — bench self-test of an **external SX1278 (SX127x)** module (`src/sx1278.{h,cpp}`, Arduino SPI; a *different* chip and register set from the WL55's internal SX126x): SPI probe (RegVersion==0x12) → 433 MHz SF9/BW125 TX beacon; **hardware-verified**. **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_node]`** — the full prototype node: DS18B20 → the shared `lora_packet.h` frame → SX1278 TX @433 (NSS=PA4 SCK=PA5 MISO=PA6 MOSI=PA7 RESET=PB0 DIO0=PB1); **hardware-verified**. It pairs with **`lora-pi-receiver/`** (SX1278 = 433 MHz, so it does **not** reach the 923 MHz WL55 gateway — a separate prototype path while WL55 boards are on back-order). **`[env:bluepill_f103c8_node_standalone]`** is the same without semihosting so it runs autonomously on any USB/battery power (the semihosting `BKPT` faults with no debugger attached; `out()` falls back to a harmless UART `Serial`). **End-to-end HARDWARE-VERIFIED (2026-08):** F103 node → SX1278 433 → Pi SX1278 (SPI) → `lora-pi-receiver/receiver.py` → web-server accepted (HTTP 2xx), dashboard `water-temp-01` `temp_hot`/`temp_cold` matched the node exactly at RSSI ≈ −64 dBm / SNR ≈ 13 dB. Python decode is **unit-tested** by `lora-pi-receiver/test_lora_packet.py`, whose vectors are generated by compiling the firmware's own `lora_packet.h` on the host (v1/v2/v3, negative temps, invalid-probe sentinel, every single-byte corruption).

  **Probe cable runs (design point 5–10 m, was 20 m):** plain 1-Wire handles it (Maxim **AN148**) — **not** RS-485 (a MAX485 *cannot* sit on the 1-Wire line: it is a directional differential UART transceiver, 1-Wire is bidirectional open-drain reversing direction mid-bit-slot). Fit an **external 4.7 kΩ** pull-up — at 10 m of Cat5 the line reaches VIH in ~3 µs against the 9 µs `ow_read_bit()` allows between release and sample, so 4.7k is sufficient and the board uses ONE pull-up value; 3.3k only for shielded cable at 10 m, 2.2k only past ~15 m (not the internal ~40 kΩ). **Twisted-pair DQ+GND**, prefer plain UTP over shielded (roughly half the pF/m), keep 3-wire VDD power; no firmware timing change. Escalate to a **DS2483/DS2480B** 1-Wire line-driver IC (active pull-up), or a remote MCU digitizing + RS-485/Modbus, only past ~30 m. See `README.md` → *Probe cable runs*. **Note the two different pull-up rules on this board and do not cross them:** 1-Wire DQ lines are **4.7k**, I2C downstream branches are **2.2k**.
